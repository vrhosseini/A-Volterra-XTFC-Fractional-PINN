A Volterra–XTFC formulation is developed for the numerical solution of Caputo-type time-fractional partial differential equations. The Caputo operator is first transformed into an equivalent Volterra integral equation, and the resulting history term is discretized by piecewise-linear spline and cubic-Hermite interpolation. Under appropriate regularity assumptions, the corresponding quadrature approximations are second- and fourth-order consistent, respectively. Initial and boundary conditions are incorporated analytically through the eXtended Theory of Functional Connections, so that the neural approximation is constructed in a constraint-satisfying space and the optimization is driven solely by the discrete Volterra residual. The method is examined for linear and nonlinear problems over \(0<\alpha\leq2\), including sub-diffusive, super-diffusive, integer-order, mixed-constraint, and oscillatory cases. Numerical results show consistently lower relative and pointwise errors than conventional fPINN formulations, with the Hermite discretization providing increased accuracy for strongly time-dependent solutions.
